Seating plan helps teachers build a classroom seating chart quickly: place your students automatically according to your own rules, then fine-tune by hand with a simple drag and drop.

Everything works OFFLINE, right on your device. No data is sent over the Internet: your students' information stays with you.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Design your room
• Start from a ready-made template — Rows, U-shape or Groups (tables of 4 or 6, in one or several rows) — or from a blank page, then adjust cell by cell.
• Add horizontal or vertical aisles to separate two seats that touch on the grid.
• Rotate each seat to reflect the real orientation of the tables (groups, U-shape…).
• The first row is the "front", on the board side.
• Save a layout under a name to reuse it in another class; an already saved room can also be updated. A layout is suggested as soon as you create a class.

Manage your students
• Add them one by one or import a list.
• For each student: first name, last name, gender, level (low / average / high), energy (calm / moderate / restless), height (short / medium / tall), poor eyesight, and free-form notes.

Set your rules (per student or per pair)
• Fixed seat: pin a student to a specific seat.
• Near the board: keep a student close to the board, within N rows (eyesight, hearing, mobility…).
• Keep apart: two students will never be neighbours.
• Keep together: two students placed next to each other.
• Each rule is either mandatory or a simple preference.

Aim for balance across the whole class
• Mix genders: avoid neighbours of the same gender.
• Mix levels: don't create two Low neighbours or two High neighbours.
• Separate restless students: avoid restless neighbours.
• Poor eyesight: move the students concerned closer to the board (front half).
• Avoid a tall student blocking a short one's view: in front of them, or beside them on an arm of the U.

Generate and adjust
• The plan is calculated automatically to respect your constraints as well as possible.
• A report shows which rules and goals are met, and which cannot be.
• Students affected by a problem are highlighted right on their seat; tap any seat to see all its details (full name, attributes, reasons).
• Rearrange freely by drag and drop, then "Validate" to check that rules and balance are still respected.
• Not happy? "Regenerate" proposes a new layout.

Archive and export
• Archive a plan under a name ("Term 1"), view it later or restore it as a new class.
• PDF export to print or share the plan.
• Export a class to a file and import it elsewhere. It contains student names: share it only with authorised people.

Getting started
• Guided tutorial on a temporary demo class, and an offline FAQ.

Readable on any screen
• The whole room is displayed, in portrait (pinch to zoom, mouse wheel on a computer, or zoom buttons) as well as in landscape.
• Each student's first name is shown whenever the seat allows it, otherwise unambiguous initials (two students with the same initials stay distinguishable).
• The colours of the boy/girl marker shown on each seat can be customised (5 palettes to choose from) in the settings.
• Light, dark or automatic theme, selectable in the settings.

PRIVACY
• 100% offline, data stored on the device (unless you export it).
• No account, no ads, no tracking.
• No Internet access permission.

Ideal for primary, middle and high school teachers who want to save time at the start of the year or reorganise a class in minutes.
